# Appendix C — The Quadruplicate Civic-Intelligence Companion {#sec:quadruplicate}

## What the Quadruplicate Is

The Quadruplicate is an open civic-intelligence platform for Crescent City,
California — a real-time system that integrates municipal-code scraping from
ecode360, source-health monitoring across eighteen government and research
sources, multi-channel alert analytics (NOAA, USGS, CDFW), LLM-powered RAG
chat, YouTube monitoring, triplicate.com news extraction, and geo-intelligence
export into a single coherent interface [@quadruplicate_intel]. Its name
reflects four analytical aims: *monitor, understand, act, and inform* — the
four-part intelligence cycle that the platform operationalizes for a small city
confronting Cascadia subduction-zone hazard, rust-to-resilience economic
transition, bounded land base, and layered institutional governance.

The platform openly publishes its source code and civic-intelligence data at
`https://github.com/docxology/crescent-city-intel` [@quadruplicate_github], and
its public snapshot is continuously available at `https://quadruplicate.org`.

## Why This Appendix Exists

This manuscript — *Crescent City in Living Waves* — provides a scholarly
foundation of approximately 46 topical chapters across four part-views (Space,
Time, People, Ideas). The Quadruplicate surfaces geo-intelligence markers and
hazard-domain tags that link directly to these manuscript chapters, so that a
reader viewing a tsunami-inundation zone on the platform can jump to the
relevant historical and scientific analysis, and a reader of the manuscript can
see which civic-intelligence domain each chapter informs.

The table below maps each of the twelve Quadruplicate civic-intelligence domains
to the manuscript chapters that provide scholarly evidence, context, and
analysis for that domain's topics. The mapping is maintained canonically in the
project's `src/domains/scholarly_context.ts` registry and is the same source of
truth that powers the Quadruplicate's geo-intelligence cross-referencing.

## Usage Guide

The Quadruplicate and this manuscript are designed for reciprocal
cross-reference. Three recommended entry points:

1. **From Quadruplicate to manuscript.** When viewing any geo-intelligence
   marker or domain page on `https://quadruplicate.org`, look for the
   "Scholarly Context" panel, which lists relevant manuscript chapters with
   their stable `{#sec:…}` anchors. Open the PDF of *Crescent City in Living
   Waves* (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20286171) and navigate to the listed section for
   the full scholarly treatment.

2. **From manuscript to Quadruplicate.** When reading a chapter that references
   a civic-intelligence domain (hazard, planning, infrastructure, or
   demographics), open the Quadruplicate's domain dashboard at
   `https://quadruplicate.org/domains/{domain-id}` for the real-time municipal
   code snapshot, alert analytics, and monitoring status that complement the
   chapter's historical analysis.

3. **By domain.** Use the table above to find all chapters that inform a
   specific domain. This is the recommended route for civic planners, emergency
   managers, and researchers working on a single thematic area — for example,
   a tsunami preparedness campaign would consult the Emergency Management row
   and read the eleven listed chapters for the complete picture.

## Technical Note

The Quadruplicate's source-of-truth domain registry lives in the
`crescent-city-intel` repository at
`https://github.com/docxology/crescent-city-intel`. The scholarly cross-reference
is defined in `src/domains/scholarly_context.ts`, which maps each manuscript
chapter anchor to one or more domain IDs, hazard-domain tags, and geographic
features. This file is the authoritative link layer between the scholarly
monograph and the operational intelligence platform. Changes to the manuscript's
chapter structure — new sections, renumbering, or retitling — should be
reflected in `scholarly_context.ts` to keep the cross-reference current.
